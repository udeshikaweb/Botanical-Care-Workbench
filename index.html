Golden Rule · Lighting First

Always consider lighting needs first, then water. Placing a desert cactus in dim shade while watering on a fixed calendar is the fastest path to root rot.

Clinical Touch & Visual Reference · Thirsty vs. Overwatered Always check bottom moisture before watering
Condition Stem / Pad Appearance Touch & Skewer Test Immediate Action
Well-Watered Plump, firm, healthy color Springs back when gently pressed; skewer slightly cool No watering needed; maintain bright airflow
Needs Water (Thirsty) Visibly thinner, wrinkled lower ribs or pads; dry tips Soft or leathery (not mushy); skewer clean & dry, pot very light Soak thoroughly at soil rim with room-temp water; empty saucer
Overwatered (Early Stress) Swollen, droopy, pale/yellow lower tissue; translucent look Remains indented when pressed; skewer dark/damp days later Stop watering; move to brighter airflow; verify drainage hole
Root Rot (Urgent) Black/brown soft patches at soil line; foul or musty odor Mushy stem base; black, slimy, disintegrating roots when unpotted Unpot immediately, trim all black roots, dry 1–3 days, repot in dry grit
